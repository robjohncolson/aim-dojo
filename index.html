THREE VERBS
  • STEP — the floor flashes a letter; press it. That steadies the Moonline.
  • SEND — let the link go in the gap between beats, not on one.
  • LAND — it counts on arrival, not on the trigger. Far voices need an earlier send.
THE NIGHT BREATHES
  • Echoes come in swells, then one bar of mercy where nothing new arrives and the chorus blooms. Breathe there.
  • The World Drum only changes pace between swells — never under your hands.
  • Arrive cleanly for a while and the tick thins toward the downbeat; a miss brings it back softly. The quiet means you are keeping the beat yourself.
THE BIG AMBER ONE
  • It closes a swell and it wants a figure, not a flurry — land its glows in a row: 3, 4, and the 1 that opens the mercy bar.
  • An off-figure hit costs you nothing but the beat, and letting it go costs nothing at all — it simply leaves.
THE SKY
  • Every Echo calls from the bearing of a real star that is up right now.
  • A voice you land flies home in the next gap and brightens that star in your sky — permanently. Brightened stars sing to you when you come back.
  • HOLD E and send at a clear patch of sky to mark a star, then E again to walk into the Sky Temple. If an Echo stands in front of it, the dojo says so and your link goes to the Echo.
  • The moon deals the night: one line at the threshold names the rule the sky chose. Nothing to configure.
ENDING ON PURPOSE
  • Stop sending and stepping for a dozen quiet seconds and the night bows: the last Echoes rise into the sky, the drum slows and resolves, one glyph of your own timing, one true line.
  • Change your mind in the first breath of it and play resumes with nothing lost.
  • After a bow, a NIGHT CARD is waiting by the door.
DOJO
  • MOUSE — look / aim
  • WASD — when the floor flashes, press the shown letter
  • L-CLICK — send; land it while the Echo glows
  • SEND (touch) — the same link; drag to aim
  • R-CLICK — close a sky note (in THEATRE, hold the sky still)
  • ESC — pause / resume
  • HOLD E + FIRE — mark a sky object under the reticle (it glows gold)
  • E (again, after a mark) — enter Sky Temple
SKY TEMPLE
  • Enter — from dojo: HOLD E + FIRE to mark, then E once more
  • FIRE — investigate the object under the reticle
  • SHIFT+E — free mouse for the panel; view stays put
  • T — ask the sky (needs a saved chart under CHART)
  • E — leave temple (back to dojo)
  • ESC — pause (temple restores on resume)
  • The ring of eight moons here holds the phases you have played under. It only ever fills.
